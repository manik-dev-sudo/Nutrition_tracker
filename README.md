# NutriPulse

A simple calorie and nutrition tracker designed to make logging meals effortless. Instead of searching and adding ingredients one by one, you can just speak or type what you ate in plain language (e.g., *"2 boiled eggs, a slice of whole wheat toast with peanut butter, and a cup of black coffee"*), and the app calculates the calories and nutrition breakdown for you.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Home & Quick Logging (Speak or Type)
- **Voice or Text Input**: Tap the microphone button to speak or type what you ate in your own words.
- **Smart Food Parser**: Automatically understands quantities, portions, fractions, and meal types (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack).
- **Confirmation Preview**: Lets you quickly review items, adjust portions with `+` / `-` buttons, or remove items before adding them to your log.
- **Today's Summary**: Live calorie ring (calories eaten vs. your daily target), progress bars for Protein, Carbs, Fats, and Fiber, a water tracker, and your meal-by-meal journal.

### 2. Daily Nutrition Analysis
- **Deep Component Breakdown**: Look beyond just calories—track your intake of dietary fiber, sugar, sodium, potassium, calcium, iron, and vitamins.
- **Macronutrient Split**: Visual breakdown of your daily calories coming from protein, carbs, and fats.
- **Diet Quality Score**: A simple A–D rating based on your balance of protein, fiber, sodium limits, and target calories.
- **Full Item Table**: A detailed list of everything you ate during the day with exact nutritional values.

### 3. Monthly Report & Date Explorer
- **Monthly Trend Chart**: See how your daily calorie intake compares against your goal across the entire month.
- **Interactive Calendar**: Color-coded calendar showing which days you hit your target. Click any day on the calendar to open its complete log and analysis.
- **Specific Date Picker**: Jump straight to any past date to review or edit your meals.
- **Monthly Stats & Export**: See your monthly average calories, protein intake, top consumed foods, and export your data as a CSV file.

### 4. Food Library & Custom Targets
- **Built-in Database**: Over 150 common foods with verified nutritional data.
- **Custom Foods**: Add your own recipes or packaged items with custom calories and macros.
- **Personal Goals**: Customize your daily calorie, protein, carb, fat, fiber, and water targets anytime.

---

## Getting Started

No installation or build steps are required...

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
2. Start logging your meals by voice or text.

All your logs and settings are automatically saved locally in your browser (`localStorage`).
